\section{Several equations at once}

An \texttt{align} environment with two labels gets two numbers, \eqref{eq:fusion} and \eqref{eq:bialgebra}:
\begin{align}
  \label{eq:fusion}
  \tikzfig{spider-fusion-Z} \\[3mm]
  \label{eq:bialgebra}
  \tikzfig{bialgebra-rule}
\end{align}
Rows marked with \verb|\nonumber| get no number:
\begin{gather}
  \tikzfig{had-had-cancel} \nonumber \\
  \tikzfig{ket0} \ \scalareq\ \tikzfig{ket0}
\end{gather}

\section{Tags and references inside figures}

An equation can have a \verb|\tag| instead of a number, like the gadget fusion rule \eqref{eq:gadget-fusion}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gadget-fusion}
  \tikzfig{gadget-fusion}
  \tag{gf}
\end{equation}
References inside a figure become links in the svg, also to another chapter. The equation numbers in the svg are
the same as in the html:
\begin{align*}
  \tikzfig{ket0}
  &\namedeq{\eqref{eq:spider-cap}} \tikzfig{ket0} \\
  &\namedeq{\zxsc} \tikzfig{ket0}
\end{align*}
The same figure can be used several times; it is converted only once:
\ctikzfig{spider-cap}

  Phase gadgets acting on the same qubits can be combined:

  \]
\end{theorem}
